Interests Generated by Family and Friends

Python numpy pandas

Overview

I had my family and friends fill out a form where they rank my top 17 interests 1-17. Based on these results I created this image. The larger the logo, the more interested I am in the subject.

Using python, I took the data from my survey, created a data frame with a row for each interest and its average rating. I printed the logo onto the image, sized based on the average rating.
