March Madness 2026 Predictor

RStudio Linear Regression XGBoost Random Forest

Overview

The NCAA Tournament is one of the most unpredictable events in sports. This project aimed to evaluate how different Machine Learning architectures handle the "madness." I scraped historical data and used the 2026 tournament results as the final test set to compare model accuracy across three distinct approaches.

1. Data Cleaning

Pulled college basketball data over the last 10 years from hoopR and filtered down to just the stats I wanted to collect.

2. Feature Engineering

Used the basic stats gathered to find offensive and defensive efficiencies, possesions per game, etc. Then built a master array finding cumulative sums to prevent data leakage.

3. Model Testing

Compared Linear Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost to determine the optimal balance of bias and variance.
