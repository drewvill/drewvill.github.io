NBA Player Ratings

RStudio hoopR

Overview

Standard box scores often fail to capture a player's true impact on the margin of victory. To solve this, I engineered an automated valuation pipeline that ingests raw play-by-play (PBP) data from every NBA game played the previous night to generate comprehensive efficiency ratings.

My program processes hundreds of metrics—including shooting splits, defensive statistics, and clutch-time performance—to assign each player a weighted performance score. By parsing the chronological flow of the game rather than just the totals, the algorithm accounts for contextual efficiency, rewarding players who contribute to winning basketball beyond the traditional stat sheet. The final output is an automated, high-level report designed for rapid talent evaluation.
